What we do

Work that helps leaders decide, organize and deliver.

Our assignments range from focused research and strategy work to institutional reform, program delivery and long-term capacity building.

01 · Strategy and policy

Turn broad goals into clear choices.

We help leadership teams define priorities, compare options and decide what to do next. The work can be as narrow as a single policy question or as broad as a multi-year strategy.

Institutional strategyPublic policyStrategic planningSector strategyRoadmaps

Typical questions: What should we prioritize? Which options are realistic? What needs to happen first? What resources and decisions will the plan require?

02 · Institutional reform

Improve the way institutions are set up and run.

Policies and programs depend on institutions that have clear mandates, workable processes and accountable decision making. We help diagnose what is getting in the way and redesign the parts that need to change.

Institutional reviewsGovernanceMandates and rolesOperating modelsProcess redesign

Typical questions: Who should own what? Where are decisions getting stuck? Which processes add value and which simply add time? What changes can the institution absorb?

03 · Program design and delivery

Turn a plan into work that can be managed.

A strategy needs owners, milestones, resources, reporting and a way to solve problems as they appear. We help clients design those systems and, where needed, support the delivery team as the work moves.

Program designDelivery unitsPMO supportResults frameworksImplementation reviews

Typical questions: Who owns each result? How will progress be measured? What should leadership see every month? Which risks need decisions now?

04 · Research and analysis

Build enough evidence to make the decision well.

We combine desk research, data analysis, interviews, workshops and field work to understand the issue from more than one angle. The output is designed around the decision, not around producing more pages.

Economic researchPolicy researchMarket studiesStakeholder analysisFeasibility studies

Typical questions: What do we know? What do we still need to know? Which assumptions matter most? What evidence would change the decision?

05 · Organization and performance

Make priorities visible in day-to-day work.

We help organizations connect strategy to structures, processes, performance measures and management routines. The aim is not constant reorganization. It is clearer accountability and better use of time and resources.

Organization designPerformance managementManagement systemsProcess improvementChange support

06 · Capacity building

Build skills around the work people actually have to do.

We design training, tools and hands-on support around real responsibilities. That can include workshops, playbooks, coaching, on-the-job support and structured knowledge transfer during a wider assignment.

Training programsLeadership developmentToolkitsCoachingKnowledge transfer

Need something different?

Start with the problem, not the service label.

Most complex assignments cut across more than one of these areas. Tell us what needs to change and we can shape the work around it.
