About ECMDC

Consulting built around clear thinking and practical work.

We help leaders understand difficult problems, make sound choices and improve the institutions responsible for delivery.

Who we are

A management consulting firm focused on institutions, policy and delivery.

ECMDC works with governments, public institutions, development partners and businesses. Our work covers strategy, public policy, institutional reform, program delivery, research, organizational performance and capacity building.

We are interested in the point where analysis has to become action. That means understanding the problem well, being clear about the trade-offs and designing recommendations around the people, budgets, rules and systems that will carry them out.

ECMDC is part of EC Group, alongside companies working in engineering, technology and data.

What matters to us

Good consulting should make the client stronger.

We judge the work by whether it helps people make better decisions and whether the answer can survive contact with real constraints.

ClarityMake the problem understandable before trying to solve it.
EvidenceUse facts that matter to the decision, not data for its own sake.
PracticalityDesign around real budgets, rules, capacity and incentives.
OwnershipBuild the answer with the people who will have to use it.
ResultsFocus on what changes, not only on what gets produced.
