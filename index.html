About ECMDC

Advice that is useful outside the meeting room.

ECMDC is a management consulting firm working across strategy, public policy, institutional reform, program delivery and organizational development.

We combine research and structured problem solving with a practical understanding of how institutions work. The aim is simple: help clients make sound decisions and carry them through.

How we work

Start with the decision that needs to be made.

We keep the work focused on what the client needs to decide, change or deliver.

1

Define the problem

Agree on the real question and the result the work needs to produce.

2

Build the evidence

Gather facts, speak with the people involved and test assumptions.

3

Design the answer

Compare options, work through trade-offs and make the plan practical.

4

Support delivery
